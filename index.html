About Akash Mohan

Welcome to my world of visual storytelling. I’m Akash Mohan, the eye behind Rover’s Tale. What began as a passion has grown into a journey of capturing life’s most beautiful moments through my lens. I believe every photograph should hold meaning—something that speaks to the heart and preserves a story worth remembering. For me, it’s not just about pictures; it’s about turning ephemeral moments into timeless memories.
